**Description:**
Facing a decision you can't see clearly? Pray this with me. 🙏

Today's verse:
"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him." — James 1:5 (KJV)

God promises to give wisdom generously to everyone who asks. This prayer asks Him to quiet the noise in your mind, to guide your work, family, money and words, to keep you from rushing ahead in fear or pride, and to lead you one step at a time.
