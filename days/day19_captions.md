# Prayer for Courage (Joshua 1:9)

## YouTube (16:9)

**Title:**
Prayer for Courage | When You Feel Afraid (Joshua 1:9)

**Description:**
Facing something that scares you? God has not left you to face it alone. Pray this with me. 🙏

Today's verse:
"Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest." — Joshua 1:9 (KJV)

Courage is not the absence of fear; it is trusting that God goes with you. This prayer asks for strength to face the conversations and decisions you have been avoiding, to silence the voice of doubt, and to take the next step in faith before you can see the whole path.
